About Precision Sequence

Precision Sequence supplies research-grade peptides for laboratory investigation. We exist because too many suppliers in this space operate with minimal transparency around purity, identity, and sourcing.

Our approach is straightforward: source from established manufacturers that meet rigorous quality standards, verify every batch independently, and make the data available. The amino acid sequence is the product. The certificate of analysis is the proof.

We do not make medical claims. We do not sell for human or animal consumption. All products are labeled and sold for research use only. Researchers are responsible for compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in their jurisdiction.

If a batch does not meet our purity and identity specifications, it is not sold. That standard is non-negotiable.
