Quality Standards

Every peptide we supply is manufactured and tested to exacting research standards.

HPLC Purity Verification

All peptides are purified by reverse-phase high-performance liquid chromatography (RP-HPLC). Our specification requires ≥99% purity by peak area. Results are documented on each Certificate of Analysis.

Typical CoA includes: HPLC chromatogram, purity %, retention time, and molecular weight confirmation.

Mass Spectrometry Identity

Identity is confirmed by mass spectrometry (ESI-MS or MALDI-TOF). The observed molecular ion matches the theoretical mass of the stated amino acid sequence. No substitutions or truncations are accepted.

Third-Party Testing

Certificates of Analysis are generated by independent, accredited laboratories. Batch-specific reports are available upon request or published for transparency. We do not rely solely on manufacturer claims.

Storage & Handling

Lyophilized peptides are stored at -20°C, protected from light and moisture. Orders ship in cold-chain packaging when appropriate to maintain compound integrity.
